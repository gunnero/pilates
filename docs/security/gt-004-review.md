## Scope and limits

This is a focused local source/behavior review, not a production penetration test or certification of vendor/host internals. Native schema-owner/operator capabilities are trusted and can bypass DDL protections. Already-running reads may complete on request state; future scheduling writes must re-resolve authority and eligibility within the shared lock. No scheduling/assignment endpoint exists, so a UI assignable flag is informational only.

Current trainer responses are private staff representations including role, staff status and membership UUID. A future member-facing response must independently select and approve display name/bio and omit staff authority details. Photo storage, publication/consent, retention/export/erasure and production telemetry policy remain deferred. Authorized staff can read operational text entered by managers; the UI discourages sensitive details but does not perform content classification.

There is no automatic owner/admin/trainer adoption. Existing grants are preserved, and teaching status never changes staff access. Role and operational capability remain independent. No supported-path boundary control was weakened to make tests pass.
